# How to upload BL1 to the OUT endpoint to boot BL1

first, you will have to acquire a valid and signed bl1.img. you will likely find it in image-*.zip in your google factory image.
to upload it(USB BOOT) is not the most complex ever. once you have acquired your bl1.img, make sure you are in the same directory as it. then simply hold down the power button of your phone, then you will just need to run `sudo dd if=bl1.img of=/dev/ttyACM0 bs=512`. you can then confirm that it worked using `sudo cat /dev/ttyACM0` while just bl1 shouldnt solve a fully corrupt phone, it wont fully boo because of one command. when you cat /dev/ttyACM0, it should be empty if you didnt release the power button. if it says bl1 header fail and irom_booting failure just like the [send data example](https://github.com/Munkenhagg/Google-Tensor-low-level-documentation/blob/main/bootROM/How-to-upload-BL1.md) then you might have a too old version of your firmware downloaded, or firmware for the wrong phone. here you can see [codenames for all pixel phones from pixel 6 to pixel 10 pro](https://github.com/Munkenhagg/Google-Tensor-low-level-documentation/blob/main/assets/codenames.md)
